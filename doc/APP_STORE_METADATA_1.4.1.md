## Product page

Promotional text:

```text
Play your own Minecraft client from your phone with live video, touch controls, chat, Data Saver, and quick QR pairing.
```

Description:

```text
MonkeyCraft lets you control your own Minecraft client from an iPhone.

Install the MonkeyCraft Fabric mod on your desktop Minecraft client, start the local server, then connect from the app by scanning the QR code or entering the connection details manually. Your Minecraft view streams to your phone and the app sends touch controls back to your client.

Features:

- Live Minecraft video streamed to your phone
- Touch controls for movement, looking, jumping, sneaking, clicking, and hotbar selection
- Dedicated chat screen with rich Minecraft chat formatting
- Server picker support when your Minecraft client is at the title screen
- Data Saver mode for lower bandwidth sessions
- Timed notifications and countdowns for supported in-game events
- Optional OpenAudioMc and MCParks audio session routing
- Password-protected local/private-network connection

MonkeyCraft is designed for users who already run Minecraft Java Edition with Fabric. It does not provide Minecraft, a hosted game server, or a public game service. Network access is user-provided over localhost, local Wi-Fi, or a private network such as Tailscale.
```
